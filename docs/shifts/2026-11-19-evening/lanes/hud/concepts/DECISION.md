# Split Ticket

19 November 2026. Both boards were generated with built-in ImageGen before implementation. They are concept art.

**Split Ticket, chosen.** A runner gets a paper race number and a large action cue at the course edge. The ink timing slip gives the split and best their own reading order. Punches count cleared checkpoints. At the finish the slip opens into a results docket. The warm stock and deep outline belong with the ink of the course and with the workbench's printed controls. Use the shape, hierarchy and contrast from the board. All numbers, moves and stage names in the game come from the pack.

**Glass Rail, rejected.** The continuous glass strip keeps the skyline clear below it, but the next move has the same visual weight as five other fields. The eye must search along the strip to find the verb. Its continuous geometry also breaks as soon as the player moves one field to another corner.

The two JPGs are the original board compositions. See PROMPTS.txt for the generation briefs.
