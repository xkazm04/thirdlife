N3 round 3: objects sinking or rising when a venue changes between its mid and far picture (owner finding 1, "objects
blinking below/above the ground", at the distance level where it is proved)

How it was found: Astra (gpt-6.2-astra, read-only, 177k tokens) listed every remaining distance- or camera-dependent
mechanism in the landscape and draw code; each was then checked here. This one is measured.
Cause: engine/kernel/world/draw/draw_pictures.gd:393-430 (`_append`, grid branch) with draw_base.gd:24 far_cell 6.0:
  the far picture re-meshes a pack's own ground on a 6 m grid, every vertex of a cell snapped to the FIRST vertex met
  there (its height too), while everything standing on that ground keeps its height. Measured in a window with an
  instrument in `_append` (removed): seed_drive's far ground moved by up to 2.07 m (2871 vertices snapped); paddle,
  wall, parkour 0.00 m (flat pads); walker, car, hockey, kit, sites have no own ground in the far picture. So on the
  drive's hollow, things sank into or floated over the ground each time the venue crossed 210/270 m from the camera.
Fix: far_cell 0.0 (the "kept as it is" setting the code already had): the far picture keeps the pack's ground exactly
  as the mid picture has it. The picture cache key includes far_cell, so old far pictures are rebuilt, not reused.
  FAR.md's far row says so.
Evidence: drive_mid_vs_far_before_after.jpg (forced mid and far pictures, two crane shots at 250-280 m, before and
  after). Honest reading: at 250-280 m the 2 m shows as a few pixels; the measured figure is the evidence, the sheet
  shows nothing else moved.
Selftest 770 n3.far_ground: a rolling 100 m ground sheet merged by the draw layer's own `_append` into mid and far:
  far ground within 0.05 m of mid under 400 points: 0.00 m now; seeded red with far_cell 6: 0.21 m (1328 far triangles
  against 4988).
Cost: the far picture of a pack with own ground keeps its ground triangles (synthetic sheet: 4988 instead of 1328);
  one merged mesh per pack, so no draw call is added.
