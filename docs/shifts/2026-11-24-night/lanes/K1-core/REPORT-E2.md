THE POINT
A user's rink game is a cartridge on the core plus a skin. The Foreman can now check a cartridge (and its rules
sidecar) exactly as the core will, change it by a small change set, and measure what a person will meet, all before
anybody plays it. The designer and the builder have a catalog of every key in player words.

2. foreman/src/cartridge.ts (+ cartridge_corpus.ts, src/test/cartridge.test.ts):
   - validateCartridgeText / validateCartridge: thirdjam's validator.
   - validateRulesText: the tl.rink.rules/1 sidecar as native/glue/rink_rules.cpp reads it. The known switches are read
     from the patch files (`if (K == "...")`). Level tunes are checked for ApplyDifficultyTune's syntax; WHICH tune keys
     exist is left to the core (130+ names; an unknown one fails at the core's reset).
   - applyChanges(base, {"game.skater.speed": 13, ...}): applies a change set to a copy, says "path: before -> after",
     and validates. null removes a key; missing objects are created on the way. parsePath handles "teams[0].colour_hex".
   - PARITY, both ways: the corpus is 106 cases: 26 shipped cartridges (regular, the 12 modes, 2 parked, the test cuts,
     thirdlife), 34 edits x 2 bases (hockey.regular and Pond: in-range edits, out-of-range, wrong types, unknown keys and
     blocks, null, seconds keys, list limits, bad colour, long title; text-level: trailing comma, duplicate key, comment,
     not JSON, empty, BOM) and 12 rules files.
   - The C++ verdicts were recorded from the built core: tools/rink_validate.gd runs RinkCore.load_cartridge, and for
     rules files RinkCoreThirdlife.load_rules + reset. They are in foreman/src/test/fixtures/cartridge-cpp-verdicts.json
     (with each case's sha1).
   - Result: 106 of 106 verdicts equal (43 accepted, 63 refused). The test says how to re-record when the corpus changes.
     foreman cartridge tests 6/6.
   - "speeds x3" (speed 33) is REFUSED by the schema (max 16). The broken-feeling proof below uses in-range values.

4. rink.sh check-cartridge <file> [--rules <file>] [--seeds N] [--plan-seeds M] [--json-only]
   - A block at the end of rink.sh; it runs lab/check_cartridge.mjs (new; nothing of K8's restructured).
   - Steps: thirdjam's validator (refused = exit 1 with the errors); then the lab (rinklab.exe, rinklab-thirdlife.exe with
     a rules file) in a scratch content dir; then plain words per level plus a tl.rink.check/1 JSON line.
   - Hands: thirdjam's regular playtester with sprint (10 seeds at each of rookie, pro and all_star) and K8's planning
     hand (2 seeds a level). About 15 s.
   - Warnings, as words: unwinnable at rookie, trivial at all_star, no goals or a flood, very long or short matches, a
     loose puck, long carries, a knockdown fest, few touches; and any figure beyond a quarter past the range thirdjam's 21
     shipped cartridges span (lab/check_cartridge.bands.json, made by lab/check_cartridge_bands.mjs in 84 s).
   - Proven:
     | cartridge | wins (rookie / pro / all_star) | at pro | warnings |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | regular | 10/10, 7/10, 6/10 | 4.3 goals, 2.9 min | none |
     | Pond | 9/10, 5/10, 5/10 | 11.3 goals, 3.8 min | none |
     | "broken feel" (speed 16, accel 36, turn 12, puck 60, friction 0, boards 1; VALID) | 10/10, 4/10, 3/10 | 1.5-minute matches | 4: mean skating speed about 15 m/s at every level, top 29.9 m/s, above anything thirdjam ships (6.2..10.3, 13..26.2) |
     | thirdlife + rules | (with rules) | 0 walked goals at every level | without the rules: 4, 1, 1 walked goals |
   - Hand note: without sprint, thirdjam's regular playtester wins 10 of 20 at pro on regular; with sprint 17 of 20
     (PLAY.md's 75.5 % is the sprint hand). check-cartridge uses the sprint hand and says so.
