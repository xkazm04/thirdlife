## The pause card: the decision (asked)
thirdjam's pause card is the game's own (Esc -> SetGamePaused + its card: PAUSED, PERIOD n - LEVEL (YOUR LEVEL), RESUME,
CHANGE A RULE, RESTART, CONTROLS, QUIT TO MENU, the stick lines, the key hints; DirectorsCallWidget.cpp:174-252). In our exe
the kernel's dungeon frame takes Esc first (dungeon.gd:220-260: set_input_as_handled, pauses the tree, its own card with
leave); under `tl play` Esc ends the run (session.gd:229-235). A pack never sees Esc and has no pause hook, and TLHud has no
focusable items. By the source, thirdjam's card is what a player meets, so it should replace the frame's card for this game.
What the kernel's frame would need: (a) a pack-declared pause (`pack.json` "pause": "pack", or a `_pack_pause(on)` callback)
so the frame pauses the tree and hands the card to the pack instead of drawing its own; (b) the pack's HUD layer drawing and
reading keys while the tree is paused (TLHud processing always; Up / Down / Enter / Esc for the items); (c) the frame's leave
as a call the pack's QUIT TO MENU makes (`ctx.leave()`), and RESTART through the core's `restart()`. Not built this round.

## Deviations (new this round)
1. The end card's wait is 114 frames, not 1.9 s of wall clock (time is physics frames here).
2. The booth's stream is a seeded RandomNumberGenerator (thirdjam: PCG32 stream 91 salted with the clock: never reproducible).
3. No barks: the booth's bark yield and bark cuts (ObserveBark) never trigger; the bark lane is not drawn.
4. Game-mode events not sent to the booth: period_end and clock (timer mode only; the regular cartridge is first_to),
   season_take, directors_call, announce, pickup and zone events (not in the regular cartridge).
5. The caption's CaptionPlace yields only to the human's figure box (not to downed figures' boxes, nor to the net / puck labels
   of HUDOneLine.cpp:88-114).
6. The pause card is not built (above).
