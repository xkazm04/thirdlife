# Concept images for Third Life's shortlisted games

Third Life is a sandbox of small 3D games (Godot engine). Its best game so far is HOKEJ, an arcade ice hockey game:
stylised toy-like figures with felt-and-clay surfaces, a warm wooden barn, readable chunky HUD, bright team colours.
Look at `ref_hokej_strip.jpg` and `ref_hokej_top.jpg` in this folder: that is the quality the engine reaches today.

For EACH game in your list make TWO images with your image generation tool (image_gen), landscape 16:9, at least
1536 px wide:
1. `<slug>_concept.png` — ART CONCEPT: a key-art / concept painting that sets the game's visual identity, mood,
   characters and world. Your own art direction: choose what makes this game distinctive and appealing.
2. `<slug>_screenshot.png` — BEST-CASE SCREENSHOT: a realistic simulated in-game screenshot of the finished game at its
   best, as a player would see it during play (game camera, HUD, score/timer, controls hints), stylised real-time 3D
   that a small team could achieve in a modern engine — the target to build toward, a clear step above the hokej
   reference, not a film still.

Rules: every design is ORIGINAL — no existing game's characters, logos, names, UI or levels; no real brands; no text
other than the game's own short HUD words. Write files ONLY into this folder. Do not touch anything else.
When done, list the files you wrote, one line each with a sentence on the art direction you chose.
