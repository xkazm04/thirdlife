# Third Life art direction · Godot 4 Forward+

## 1. Painted storybook

- Light the road and left-facing slopes with low apricot sun. Lift the shadow sides with peach bounce near the ground and lilac fill from the sky.
- Paint broad translucent washes into the sky and cloud textures. Break the meadow and ochre road into opaque gouache strokes anchored in world space so they hold still as the camera moves.
- Break the fence and rock contours with dry-brush marks. Reserve fine sepia ink for overlaps such as the runner’s cuff against the hand.
- Keep the red jacket and indigo trousers in broad color masses. Pick out the hair tuft, collar and boot tops with a few warm strokes that read at gameplay distance.

## 2. Crisp cel

- Quantize diffuse light into two or three bands with a spatial shader ramp. Use warm yellow on sunward planes and plum in cast shadows, with a crisp boundary across the road.
- Give the runner, rails and beam even dark contours using expanded back faces. Taper outline width with distance so the crane lattice stays open.
- Set flat cream cloud shapes with a single lavender underside against a saturated violet-to-peach sky. Keep meadow texture sparse enough to preserve the pine silhouettes.
- Build the runner from distinct hair, collar, cuff and boot shapes. Keep the jacket red through both light bands and separate it from the blue trousers with one dark seam.

## 3. Stylized modern 3D

- Bevel the platform edges, round the pine tiers and soften the runner’s blocky limbs. Let smooth normals carry the light around the jacket collar and sculpted hair.
- Use a low warm sun for a narrow golden rim. Bake diffuse bounce into the static landscape with LightmapGI and place probes along the road to light the moving runner.
- Keep the grass and jacket matte, with broader highlights on painted timber and a tighter reflection on the river. Use restrained ambient occlusion beneath boots and between scaffold joints.
- Separate teal pines from salmon mesas by color and depth. Rounded edges catch enough light to describe the silhouettes without an ink pass.

## 4. Toy diorama

- Shape the red jacket, blue trousers and hair as separate pieces of painted clay. Use shallow fingerprint normals and sparse chipped paint at cuffs and boot edges.
- Wrap the pine tiers in rough felt, run fine wood grain along each scaffold dowel and give the resin river a smooth reflective surface. Slightly uneven cream stripes reveal the beam’s brushwork.
- Rake warm light across the fibers and tool marks. Use soft shadows with tight contact darkening where the runner’s boots and the scaffold feet meet the ground.
- For a tilt-shift miniature effect, keep the runner and near end of the beam in focus. Ease depth-of-field blur onto the closest rail and distant meadow while preserving the crane silhouette.
