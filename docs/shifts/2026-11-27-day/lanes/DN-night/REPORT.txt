LANE DN: DAY AND NIGHT IN THE HOME (27 Nov 2026, Opus, worktree d27-night, branch d27-night from main e3b1ccc8)

WHAT IT DOES
- The Home now has a clock. A day lasts 86,400 physics frames, which is 24 minutes at 60 Hz. The clock moves the sun
  (and at night a moon) together with the sky, fog, ambient light, the cel bands' light and shade, the water and the
  grade. It passes through dawn (06:00), day, dusk (18:00) and night.
- Noon is the style file's own values, key for key. The style only says how dawn, dusk and night differ from it. The
  colours hold noon's look from 09:00 to 15:00 and the night's look from 19:12 to 04:48.
- At the two blue hours (05:24 and 18:36) the light is flat: the lit band takes the shade's colour. The light turns
  from the sun to the moon there, so the swap is never seen. The light never goes below 4 degrees.
- Lamps: the kit's lights (street_lamp, house windows, light_mast) are off by day. At dusk they come on one by one:
  each lamp has its own threshold of 0.05 to 0.30 of the day's lamp strength, taken from where it stands. At dawn they
  go out the same way.
- The 48-light cap is now 48 LIGHTS, not 48 pieces. Every 15 physics frames the lamps that are on are sorted by
  distance to the camera and the 48 nearest get a light node. A live lamp keeps its node unless another lamp is nearer
  than 0.9 of its distance, so the choice does not flicker at its edge. A newly lit node fades in over 12 frames.
- Lit pieces are now drawn instanced like every other piece (build/piece_draw.gd). Their kit lights became
  "sockets" of home/day_lamps.gd.
- Keys, in build mode only:
    N  jump to the next of dawn, noon, dusk, night
    H  hold the clock, or let it run again
  Neither key collides with build mode, any tool, visit.gd or boot: checked, and case home.daynight.keys asserts it.
  Neither key does anything while a tool has the keyboard or a text field has focus. While building, a label at the
  top right reads "18:00  dusk    N next · H hold".
  The keys are in BUILD.md's key table.
- Settings: settings.json, schema tl.home-settings/1, in the Home's build folder (user://home, or
  TL_HOME_BUILD_DIR). It is never written into build.json. Shape:
    {"schema":"tl.home-settings/1","day":{"frame":0..86399,"cycle":"run"|"hold"}}
  Unknown keys are errors. A file that cannot be read is never written over. The file is saved on N and H, every
  3,600 frames while the clock runs, and on quit. A fresh Home starts at noon with the clock running.
- A game on stage: the clock holds and the look eases to noon over 60 frames. Off the stage the clock runs again.
  Dungeons are a separate scene with their own look. When the Home goes, TLStyle.load_style of the same file writes the
  style's own values back.

THE CHOICE ON "NOTHING ELSE CHANGES" (brief item 4): BOTH, AND PROVED
(a) The cycle is OFF unless a person visits the Home in a window, meaning build mode opened a folder: a person's walk,
    or a case's open(). Scripted runs, tours and headless runs write nothing and the clock stands at noon.
    Headless, the landscape grows no Home at all, so there is no clock (home.daynight.off_headless and
    home.daynight.off).
(b) The default time, noon, draws exactly today's sun:
    - Headless: day_look(0.5) equals the file key for key, and its 9 globals are byte-equal to what
      _register_globals computes (home.daynight.noon).
    - In a real window: after TLStyle.install, apply_day(noon) leaves 45 of 45 values byte-identical. These are the 9
      globals, the sun's rotation, colour and energy, the overhead key, the fog, ambient and exposure, every sky
      parameter, the grade and the water. After night and then noon again, they are still identical
      (day_case.gd --mode=window: "HOME daynight.window ok 45 values compared, 43 written at noon; differing after
      noon: []; after night and noon again: []; 32 of them move at night").
(c) The look pins stay. toon_cel.gdshader, water_cel.gdshader, every sky shader and style.json are untouched. The day
    section is only in style_crisp.json and is read only by the Home's clock. 610 celsurf, 770 n3 and style.frame.*
    are in the uncapped selftest below.
(d) No public API change. The day functions live in engine/style/day/style_day.gd, which is not indexed and not pack
    API. tl_style.gd only gained "day" in SECTIONS plus its check: 588 lines, under 600. Kernel API stays 1.31;
    tl arch PASS.

BEHAVIOUR CHANGES (named, with why)
- Lit pieces (house, street_lamp, light_mast) are no longer built as nodes. Until today the first 48 such pieces
  placed were nodes whose lights burned day and night; pieces past 48 had no light. Now every lit piece is instanced,
  and its lights are lit by time and by distance to the camera.
  - figures().nodes now counts only breakables (crates).
  - Headless nothing changes, because the kit makes no lights headless.
- By day the lamps' light pools are gone. The emissive windows still glow as before: the palette's window emit is
  untouched.
- Under TL_STYLE=classic (no day section) the lamps burn always, the nearest 48, as before. There is no night.
- The Home's look moves with the clock for a person's walk. A fresh Home starts at noon, which is today's look; dusk
  comes after 6 minutes and full night after about 7.

FOR THE OWNER: HOW TO SEE IT
Walk the Home in the exe or the editor binary. Press B, then N to jump to dusk, then N again for night; H holds the
clock. Build a few street lamps or a house (the piece tool) and walk among them at night.
Or let it run: dusk arrives 6 minutes after noon.
