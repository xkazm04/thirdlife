## Reuse decision: a kernel API addition (1.7), not a copy in the pack
- **Why not in the pack:** the walker's moves live in `kernel/world/terrain/world_body.gd`. A pack may not load `kernel/world`, and `TLBiped` had no slide and different numbers (walk 2.3, run 6.0, jump 4.5 m/s, gravity 9.8). Copying the code into the pack would split the feel into two copies that drift apart.
- **What I added, said as an API addition:** `KERNEL_API` 1.6 -> 1.7. `TLBiped.create({"feel": "walker"})` now runs the same `world_body.gd` on the biped's own capsule in the pack's own space, through a new 43-line adapter, `kernel/world/terrain/node_space.gd`. The new public members are `slide()`, `move_state()`, `move_events()`, the `feel` property and the constants `WALKER_WALK` / `WALKER_RUN`. `drive_from_input` also reads a `slide` map key. API-V1.json is regenerated and `tl arch` passes.
- **The default feel is unchanged:** hockey, seeds and world hashes are all unchanged in the gate.
- **Evidence the feel matches:** `bot_reach` measured a run of 9.00 m/s and a jump 0.944 m high with 33 ticks in the air. WORLD.md gives 0.94 m and 33 ticks.
- **One addition beyond the walker:** during a run slide the biped is half as tall. The brief assumed the run slide could get under a low bar, but the walker's slide does not lower his body, so as built it could not. Even with the duck, the step-up lift (0.55 m) means the bar must be 1.7 m clear; at 1.25 m he was blocked. The visitor in the village does not duck. Only TLBiped does.
