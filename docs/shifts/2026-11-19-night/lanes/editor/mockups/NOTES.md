# Third Life workshop

## 01 · Match length

The player's sentence stays above the question, and six skaters on the preview ice show what the Foreman understood. The suggested three 36-second periods come from the hockey base mode. The player can change either number, accept both, or leave match length open without spending anything.

## 02 · Road clearance

The 60 × 30 m ice sheet sits inside a larger outline that includes the stands. Changing the ice size updates the footprint and its road check together. Hatching shows where the proposal covers the road, while the nearby kart track, gate path and 2 m walker give it scale. The player can centre the proposal and check the fit again before choosing **Build it**. The 70 × 42 m dressed footprint is an estimate for this example.

## 03 · Build site

Each crew posts its status at its bay, so the player can see that skater checks continue while the stands crew waits. Its question points back to the scaffold and explains why the north side would keep the gate clear. The agreed match rules and latest rink picture stay in view while the player answers. Elapsed time and token spend are illustrative readings for this build.

## 04 · Arena idea

Enemy waves fall outside the available recipes because the enemies need to act on their own. The outline over the empty plot keeps the idea visible while the Foreman explains the extra time, uncertainty and questions. Offering a first decision about when the next wave starts gives the player a way to judge that planning process before committing.
