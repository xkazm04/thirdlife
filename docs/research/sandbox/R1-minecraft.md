## 1. Why people build there

**Most Minecraft building happens inside survival, not in Creative.** I found no official telemetry on time per mode. The only real survey I found (a Czech study) has 50.58 % preferring Survival ([SlideShare report](https://fr.slideshare.net/kopeckyk/the-minecraft-phenomenon-in-the-czech-environment-research-report-english-version)). A "29 % creative share" figure circulates on aggregator sites with no Mojang source **[unverified]** ([sqmagazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/minecraft-statistics/)). An older study found girls more likely to play Creative and boys Survival ([phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2018-01-minecraft-kids-tech-gender-imbalance.pdf)). The base you build to survive the night is the first building most players do. The need (shelter, storage, farms) gives building a reason before it becomes an art.

**The loops that make building matter, with evidence:**
- **Showing off and sharing.** Planet Minecraft reports about 5.75 M members, 3.51 M submissions, 4.9 B views and 767 M downloads. Its XP system rewards views, downloads, votes and favourites ([Planet Minecraft levels page](https://www.planetminecraft.com/members/levels/); undated live counter). A build is made to be seen.
- **A game on top of building.** On Hypixel, Build Battle (solo, teams, Pro mode, "Guess the Build") turns building into a timed, judged party game ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/3-best-minecraft-build-battle-servers)). Housing (since 2015) gives every player a house to decorate, then script with actions, events, regions and functions, and invite people to ([Hypixel wiki via Wikiwand](https://www.wikiwand.com/simple/articles/Hypixel); [HTSL docs](https://housingdocs.github.io/html/HTSL/Introduction.html)). Hypixel's founder claims about $1.1 B worth of Minecraft accounts have played the server in 13 years ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/minecraft/usd1-1-billion-worth-of-minecraft-accounts-have-played-the-hypixel-minecraft-server-in-the-past-13-years-founder-says-and-its-still-growing)).
- **Collective megaprojects.** BuildTheEarth is volunteer-run and, per Guinness (Oct 2026), has done "far less than 1 %" of Earth's surface, "not a race to 100 %" ([Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2026/10/worlds-biggest-minecraft-project-is-a-beautiful-labour-of-love-for-thousands-of-volunteers)). The building is the social activity.
- **Economy.** The Marketplace has paid creators more than $500 M since 2017 **[secondary]** ([Corq](https://corq.studio/insights/minecraft-marketplace-inside-the-platforms-500-million-creator-economy/)). Paid professional builders exist: Axiom sells them a separate commercial license ([Modrinth Axiom](https://modrinth.com/mod/axiom)). In June 2026 Mojang added a 5 % affiliate commission on Marketplace web sales ([NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/minecraft-developer-outlines-vision-for-a-creator-platform/)).
- **Video.** Minecraft passed 1 trillion YouTube views (2021). The most-viewed videos are music parodies and animations, not gameplay or builds ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/minecraft-videos-now-have-1-trillion-views-on-youtube-but-the-most-popular-ones-arent-actually-made-in-minecraft)). Building channels thrive on timelapses and "transformations" (fWhip, Kelpie the Fox, per [YouTube trends](https://www.youtube.com/trends/articles/minecraft-trillion)). The 2025 trend leans to drama formats (100 Days, Lifesteal) more than quiet building **[secondary]** ([EaseUS list](https://recorder.easeus.com/screen-recording-resource/best-minecraft-youtubers.html)).

**Mojang's own 2026 bet is building-as-platform.** At Minecraft Live on 26 Sep 2026 Mojang announced a "creator platform" to "get more people building experiences". It named no tools, features or timeline, and the press read it as a Roblox challenge ([NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/minecraft-developer-outlines-vision-for-a-creator-platform/); [geo.tv](https://www.geo.tv/latest/683863-minecraft-live-2026-list-of-everything-announced)). For contrast, Roblox paid creators $1.5 B in 2025 (same NetInfluencer piece).

**Hytale was pitched straight at builders and modders, and that is what landed.**
- Reviewers called creative and modding "the standout" while calling the content thin. Switchblade scored creative/modding tools 8.5/10 ("best-in-class") and value 7/10, "exceptional for builders but limited for pure adventure players" ([Switchblade](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/hytale/early-access-review-2026/)).
- PCGamesN called it "an absolute paradise for world builders and modders" even in its messy state ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/hytale/development-philosophy-long-term-vision)). AOL's review called it "basically Minecraft 2" with "very little direction" ([AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/hytale-early-access-review-far-192626446.html)).
- Mojang's Jens Bergensten played it and posted: "very polished, but when I play, I get the feeling that I'd rather just play Minecraft." He wanted "more unique solutions". Hytale's lead agreed and said Minecraft's systems had years to mesh ([Shazoo, translating his Bluesky post](https://shazoo.ru/2026/01/24/178608/kreativnyi-direktor-minecraft-poproboval-hytale-i-cestno-priznalsia-cto-predpocel-by-prosto-igrat-v-minecraft); [PC Gamer headline](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/minecrafts-lead-dev-says-hytale-is-very-polished-but-hed-rather-just-play-minecraft-and-hytale-boss-says-hes-probably-right-for-now/)).

## 2. The building primitive

**Minecraft's grain: a 1 m cube with 16x16 textures, plus a small family of partial shapes.** The family is slabs, stairs, walls and fences that connect, trapdoors and buttons used as detail. A novice gets a box. Guides agree on the tells of a beginner build:
- a flat roof (called "the number-one giveaway"),
- builds too small,
- flat walls with no depth ([Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/minecraft/best-minecraft-building-tips-tricks-beginners); [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/minecraft/4-best-ways-add-depth-minecraft-build)).

The fixes are a learned grammar: recess windows, frame walls, pitch roofs, use stairs, slabs and trapdoors for depth, add greenery. A Minecraft build looks good only after you learn it. The primitive itself does not help.

**Missing shapes are the builders' longest grievance.**
- Vertical slabs have been "one of the most requested features" for a decade ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/why-vertical-slabs-should-be-in-minecraft)). Mojang's old feedback policy excluded features that "inhibit natural creativity", and vertical slabs were the example. Jeb later did not recall that as the reason ([Recapio digest of Phoenix SC](https://recapio.com/digest/vertical-slabs-what-s-happening-here-by-phoenix-sc); [r/Minecraft thread](https://redlib.hbubli.cc/r/Minecraft/comments/1hblfwc/mojang_no_vertical_slabs_also_mojang/m1hhfsu/?context=3)).
- Concrete stairs and slabs and mixed slabs are the next asks ([feedback.minecraft.net](https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/community/posts/44485773275021-Add-Concrete-Slabs-and-Stairs-more-general-purpose-varieties)).
- Wool stairs and slabs only arrived in 2026 (26.3) ([Windows Central](https://windowscentral.com/gaming/minecraft/minecrafts-next-update-is-revealed-bringing-with-it-wool-stairs-straw-beds-and-a-brand-new-biome)).

**Tedium at scale is the second grievance.** Vanilla Java offers only `/fill`, `/clone` and structure blocks. Players on Bedrock consoles and phones cannot mod, which drives "Builder Mode" requests on Mojang's feedback site ([feedback.minecraft.net](https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/community/posts/46445679519757-Builder-Mode-for-Minecraft-Java-and-Bedrock)). Terrain is the worst: serious map makers leave the game for WorldPainter, often fed heightmaps from Gaea or World Machine ([WorldPainter](https://worldpainter.net); [polystruct guide](https://polystruct.github.io/creative/worldpainter/generation.html); [Gamepressure](https://gamepressure.com/newsroom/heres-how-those-impressive-minecraft-maps-are-made/z1264e)). That is a heightfield workflow.

**The escape tools and their adoption** (download counters, 27 Nov 2026):

| Tool | What it does | Adoption |
|---|---|---|
| WorldEdit | selections, brushes, schematics, scripting | 67.4 M CurseForge ([CF](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/worldedit)), 12.1 M Modrinth ([MR](https://modrinth.com/mod/worldedit)) |
| Axiom | in-game editor: magic/boolean selection, heightmap tools, sculpt, smooth/distort; free non-commercial, paid commercial license | 14.5 M Modrinth ([MR](https://modrinth.com/mod/axiom)) |
| Litematica | schematic *projection* (a ghost you build into), creative helpers | 28.6 M Modrinth ([MR](https://modrinth.com/mod/litematica)) |
| Chisels & Bits | carve blocks into 1/16 bits | 143.8 M CurseForge ([CF](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/chisels-bits)); inflated by modpack installs, my inference |
| Create | "aesthetic technology": decorative machines | 28.5 M Modrinth ([MR](https://modrinth.com/mod/create)) |
| WorldPainter | external heightfield and material painter | no counter found |

Axiom and WorldEdit are used together, not as rivals ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/axiom-vs-worldedit-which-minecraft-mod-better-you)). Mojang's answer on Bedrock is **Editor**, now v1.0. It is a built-in editor on Windows with selection, cloning, painting and script-written extensions ([minecraft.net Editor v1.0](https://minecraft.net/en-us/creator/article/editor-v1-0-is-here); [Creator Tools](https://minecraft.net/en-us/creator/tools)). It still gets tools through Preview builds: Jigsaw, Workbench and a colour-aware "Custom Mesh Tool" that places imported meshes as block groups ([Preview 26.50 notes](https://www.minecraft.net/nb-no/article/minecraft-preview-26-50-24); [26.0 changelog](https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/43274629736717-Minecraft-Bedrock-Edition-26-0-Changelog)).

**Hytale's primitive: Minecraft's cube, plus multi-cell blocks, model props and connected textures, with the WorldEdit/Axiom layer built in.**
- **At launch** ([official Creative Mode post](https://hytale.com/news/2025/11/hytale-creative-mode)):
  - a shape brush (spheres, cones, pyramids; density, wall thickness, axis lock, masks),
  - **scripted brushes** that are asset-defined and editable in a Node Editor, and paint "mountains, grassy paths, or procedurally generated ruins",
  - a prefab browser plus a separate prefab-editor world,
  - a paste tool that also works as a brush,
  - cuboid selection (non-rectangular selection and a "magic wand" were planned),
  - machinima cameras.
- **Update 5 (26 May 2026):** Sculpt with erosion and sampling (smoothing about 6x faster), Extrude merged with Flood, and placement modes Replace, Draw, Fast and Free ([Update 5 notes](https://hytale.com/news/2026/5/update-5-patch-notes)).
- **Update 6 (27 Aug 2026)** ([Update 6 notes](https://hytale.com/news/2026/8/update-6-patch-notes)):
  - a **Color Tool** with gradient and shading modes,
  - saved block-palette presets,
  - selections up to 16 M blocks,
  - undo that replays brush strokes in order,
  - `/prefabpreview` spawning a **ghost** of a prefab, plus a plugin API that reveals the ghost "layer by layer",
  - an in-game mod browser with free mods only.
- Update 4 added 500+ blocks ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/03/hytale-update-4-is-another-absolute-whopper-with-over-500-new-blocks/page=1/)).

Hytale says itself that this is an *editor* for content creators. A separate casual creative mode built on "playful interactions over editor features" is still in development ([Creative Mode post](https://hytale.com/news/2025/11/hytale-creative-mode)).

## 3. The behaviour layer

**Minecraft has four tiers, each with a steeper barrier.**
1. **Redstone.** Mojang's own "How We Make Minecraft" admits its high skill ceiling "can make it seem inaccessible to new or casual players" ([Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/how-we-make-minecraft-episode-7-discusses-redstone-and-depth-it-brings-minecraft)). Commentators note it "isn't explained well in-game" and does not follow building logic ([Muddy River News](https://muddyrivernews.com/gaming/is-redstone-minecrafts-hardest-mechanic-or-its-secret-gateway-to-engineering/20250811100859/)). The 2025 Redstone Experiments removed "locationality" (circuits behaving differently by position) and were praised for it ([GGServers](https://ggservers.com/blog/mojang-should-listen-to-redstoners-a-balanced-take-on-the-redstone-experiments/), promotional source).
2. **Command blocks and datapacks** (Java). These are now data-driven: item components replaced raw NBT in 1.20.5, a change Mojang itself called a major breaking effort for datapack and map authors ([1.20.5 notes](https://minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-java-edition-1-20-5)).
3. **Bedrock add-ons and the JavaScript Script API.** Custom Components V2 is still an experiment behind "Beta APIs", and Scripting v2 can break v1 code ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/minecraft/creator/documents/scriptingv2.0.0overview)).
4. **Java mods** (Fabric, NeoForge). Minecraft mods passed 100 B downloads **[secondary]** ([ARY](https://arynews.tv/minecraft-mods-a-milestone-in-gaming-history)).

I found no official figure for what share of players use redstone or commands. Each tier is a different language, and none of them is the building tool.

**The most telling case is Hypixel Housing.** Players script their houses with no-code actions, conditions, events, regions and functions. Power users then built **HTSL**, a text language where "each line corresponds directly to a Housing action", and import/export tools, because clicking through menus is too slow. Hypixel changed its rules to allow those macros ([HTSL docs](https://housingdocs.github.io/html/HTSL/Introduction.html); [HTSL Reborn](https://www.modpackindex.com/mod/142324/htsl-reborn)). A no-code layer grows a text form once people do real work in it.

**Hytale's answer is shapes plus rules.** Update 5's **Trigger Volume Tool** places boxes, spheres or cylinders that react to players, creatures, arrows or broken blocks. A volume fires any of 20+ effects (sound, VFX, weather, teleport, NPC spawn, prefab placement), takes conditions and delays, and saves inside prefabs. In Hytale's words, "Because they are just shapes and rules, there's no coding required!" ([Update 5](https://hytale.com/news/2026/5/update-5-patch-notes)). Update 6 added signals between tagged volumes, an "always-active" rule that grants creative-style building inside a volume, and a JSON `EncounterManager` for multi-NPC encounters ([Update 6](https://hytale.com/news/2026/8/update-6-patch-notes)). Below that sit Java server plugins and JSON assets. Mod-makers had already built "dungeon scripter" logic mods ([CurseForge LogicVisuals](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/logicvisuals-dungeon-scripter)). Hytale hired the Create mod team in April 2026 to "open the door to automation" ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/rpg/hytale-devs-hire-minecraft-modders-behind-incredible-create-mod-teasing-that-it-opens-the-door-to-automation-and-a-whole-lot-more-in-their-sandbox-rpg/)). Chapter I ships a Prop Hunt minigame ([Massively OP](https://massivelyop.com/2026/10/13/hytale-introduces-its-first-dungeon-a-multi-phase-boss-and-minigames-in-its-out-of-the-breach-update/)). Tooling for competitive minigames was not in early access at launch ([Nodecraft](https://nodecraft.com/support/games/hytale/game-information/hytale-gamemode-overview)).

## 4. The look

**Why Minecraft's look works:**
- **One grid and one texel density** make every player's build legible and roughly coherent next to every other's at a distance, whatever the skill.
- **Low detail invites imagination.** Mojang's How We Make Minecraft episode argues the graphics "encourage players to create their own beauty" ([Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/how-we-make-minecraft-episode-3-discusses-minecrafts-classic-artwork-and-design)).
- **Consistency is the rule.** Art lead Jasper Boerstra: pick one style and keep it uniform, "consistency matters most" ([minecraft.net interview](https://minecraft.net/article/try-new-minecraft-textures)).
- **It is hard to fake.** Mojang's executive producer called the look an "extra quality" that is "really tricky to create through AI" ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/gaming-industry/minecraft-developer-rejects-generative-ai-its-important-that-it-makes-us-feel-happy-to-create-as-humans)).

**Where it fails, and how players fight it.** Lighting is flat, shapes are boxy, and there is no mood. Players' answer is shaders and resource packs at huge scale: Iris, the shader loader, has 198 M Modrinth downloads ([Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/iris)), and Complementary Reimagined has 74.1 M ([Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/shader/complementary-reimagined)). Mojang's own fix, **Vibrant Visuals**, shipped on Bedrock on 17 Jun 2025 ([minecraft.net](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/chase-the-skies-and-vibrant-visuals-playable-today)). Complaints followed: an orange/yellow tint, frame-rate drops, shorter render distance and broken underwater fog ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/issues-vibrant-visuals-minecraft-bedrock)).

On Java it is still groundwork. There is a rendering refactor, and Vulkan entered testing in 26.2 snapshot 1 with an OpenGL fallback ([minecraft.net VV Java](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/vibrant-visuals-java-edition); [26.2 snapshot 1](https://minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-26-2-snapshot-1)). I found no evidence that Vibrant Visuals has shipped on Java by late 2026.

Fragmentation follows. What one Minecraft builder sees depends on edition, pack and shader. Mods such as Chisels & Bits or Create each bring their own grain. So the shared screenshot is not a shared look.

**Hytale has the clearest art bible in the genre** ([official modelling guide](https://hytale.com/news/2025/12/an-introduction-to-making-models-for-hytale)):
- cubes and quads only ("no edge loops… no triangles, pyramids");
- props and blocks at 32 px per unit, characters at 64;
- node stretch held between 0.7x and 1.3x;
- textures painted as illustrations with baked AO, tinted shadows, and no pure black or white;
- a non-PBR renderer;
- four pillars: Immersive, Fantasy, Stylized, Flexible.

Every block, prop and modded asset is made to one rule set. That is exactly the "one coherent stylised look" premise. Yet the market verdict was "too familiar". A mod that makes Hytale *look more like Minecraft* got press ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/adventure/minecraft-inspired-rpg-hytale-created-by-minecraft-multiplayer-server-devs-gets-mod-to-make-it-look-more-like-minecraft)). A coherent look is necessary, not sufficient.

## 5. AI: everything I found, official and not

**Mojang and Microsoft, official:**
- **Mojang's stance (Mar 2025).** The vanilla director was "not enthusiastic", and Microsoft clarified that Mojang "currently" has no intention of using generative AI in development ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/gaming-industry/minecraft-developer-rejects-generative-ai-its-important-that-it-makes-us-feel-happy-to-create-as-humans); [Yahoo](https://tech.yahoo.com/general/articles/mojang-wont-generative-ai-minecraft-173000716.html)).
- **Copilot in Minecraft (demo, May 2024).**
  - It read the player's inventory, walked them through crafting a sword and answered building-technique questions ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/software/ai/microsoft-demos-how-ai-which-is-famously-accurate-can-help-players-figure-out-what-to-build-in-minecraft); [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/microsoft-is-putting-its-copilot-ai-into-games-like-minecraft/1100-6523588/)).
  - It grew into "Copilot for Gaming". Fans mocked it ("People need to be guided in Minecraft?") ([Soap Central](https://soapcentral.com/humor/xbox-announces-ai-gaming-coach-copilot-fans-disbelief-people-need-guided-minecraft)).
  - **In May 2026 Xbox CEO Asha Sharma stopped the console version and began winding down the mobile one** ([Tom's Hardware](https://tomshardware.com/video-games/xbox/xbox-ceo-asha-sharma-kills-copilot-for-gaming); [Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260506-microsoft-copilot-no-longer-consoles-xbox-ceo)). TechRadar's verdict: "nobody actually wanted Copilot on Xbox" ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/xbox/microsoft-has-finally-realized-what-most-of-us-knew-all-along-nobody-actually-wanted-copilot-on-xbox)).
- **Merl (2025).** A generative AI support agent on the Minecraft Help site. It was criticised for answering "I don't know" and refusing words like "kill", with fears it would replace human support ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/minecraft/merl-support-agent-ai-beta); [Mojang help article](https://help-redesign.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/41857318106765-About-Merl-the-Minecraft-Support-Virtual-Agent)). A "Remove Merl" thread exists on the feedback site ([feedback.minecraft.net](https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/community/posts/39457476286861-Remove-Merl)).
- **Minecraft Education.** Copilot writes Minecraft lesson plans in Teach (updated 14 Apr 2026). There are also AI Foundations lessons, a world ("The First Night") about how AI agents work, and Prompt Lab ([Microsoft Education blog](https://education.minecraft.net/blog/design-personalized-lessons-with-copilot-teach); [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/embark-ai-journey-free-ai-tools-microsoft-education/build-ai-literacy-digital-citizenship-minecraft-educations-ai-foundations)). All of it is AI *about* the game for teachers, not AI building in the world.
- **Microsoft Research world models.**
  - Muse/WHAM (Feb 2025, trained on Bleeding Edge, published in Nature). Developers were wary, and Game Developer said Microsoft "doesn't really explain how it'll help developers" ([Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/introducing-muse); [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/production/microsoft-hypes-another-generative-ai-model-but-doesn-t-really-explain-how-it-ll-help-developers)).
  - **MineWorld** (Apr 2025): a 300 M-parameter real-time Minecraft world model at 4-7 fps, research only. Its checkpoints were taken down in May 2025 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08388v1); [GitHub mirror](https://github.com/sysuyy/mineworld)).
- **The backlash climate.** A March 2026 Minecraft night attraction in Buenos Aires was accused of AI visuals **[unverified, single French source]** ([Xboxygen](https://www.xboxygen.com/?p=352347)). In Feb 2026 Notch said AI is "being forced down our throats" ([Fortune](https://dc.fortune.com/2026/02/12/minecraft-billionaire-founder-marcus-persson-ai-coding-warnings-big-tech-embrace-gen-z-advice-learn-programming-foundations)).

**Third-party and research:**
- **Agents that play.**
  - Voyager (NVIDIA, 2023): GPT-4 writes JavaScript skills into a growing library, reaching 3.3x more unique items than earlier agents ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291)). It is survival, not architecture.
  - **Mindcraft** (UCI, via Mineflayer) lets any LLM drive a bot. Its MineCollab paper concludes LLM agents are "ill-optimized for multi-agent collaboration, especially in embodied scenarios" ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.17950v1); [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mindcraft-bots/mindcraft/develop/README.md)). Community "Andy" fine-tunes exist; the 9B Andy-4.2 claims full diamond armour unaided **[vendor claim]** ([Featherless](https://featherless.ai/models/Mindcraft-CE/Andy-4.2)).
  - The most-adopted AI mod I found is **Player2 AI NPC**, an LLM companion that does tasks for you, at about 6.6 M CurseForge downloads ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/player2npc)). PC Gamer's framing of a similar mod: "no more strip mining or wood gathering ever again" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/this-minecraft-mod-lets-you-command-steve-to-do-whatever-you-want-which-means-no-more-strip-mining-or-wood-gathering-ever-again)).
- **Text-to-build mods, all bring-your-own-key, none with visible adoption** **[adoption unverified]**:
  - AIbuilder (Fabric) turns AI output into a `.litematic` and *loads it as a Litematica placement*, a ghost you then build into ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/aibuilder)).
  - Construct uses Gemini on 1.21.11 ([Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/project/a6RC0vpD)).
  - AIConstructor is a Paper plugin for Gemini, Claude, OpenAI or Ollama that builds from text or a photo ([Hangar](https://hangar.papermc.io/alanlapid/AIConstructor)).
  - MineDit offers one-shot, staged and agent modes ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minedit)).
  - BuilderGPT exports `.schem` and `.mcfunction` ([GitHub](https://github.com/CubeGPT/BuilderGPT)).
  - Players also post "AI-assisted build generation" requests to Mojang ([feedback](https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/community/posts/39337982501517-AI-ASSISTED-BUILD-GENERATION)).
- **Benchmarks that use Minecraft as a judge of AI building.**
  - **MC-Bench** (2025): models write code that builds a prompt, and humans vote blind on pairs. Claude 3.5/3.7 Sonnet led early on. Its creator argues visual judgment beats reading code ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/20/a-high-schooler-built-a-website-that-lets-you-challenge-ai-models-to-a-minecraft-build-off)).
  - **VoxelBench/MineBench** (Jul 2026): models emit raw JSON block coordinates, with GPT-5.5 at its top compute setting leading. The same article cites VoxelCodeBench (220 tasks): "producing executable code was far easier than producing spatially correct outputs", and "geometric construction and multi-object composition were the hardest categories, for every model tested" ([Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/gpt-55-topped-a-minecraft-building-benchmark-and-the-spatial-reasoning-implications-go-far-beyond-gaming/)).
- **GDMC settlement generation (IEEE CIS) has the strongest evidence for us.** The 2026 winners (Leiden) tried LLMs "in a relatively naive manner", but the output "made no sense". One said: "The models really seemed to struggle with reasoning about three-dimensional space." They won with **hand-made building templates** placed by algorithms that read the terrain: roads follow valleys and rivers, and houses adapt to biome, with desert houses losing water features and jungle houses gaining vines ([Leiden University](https://www.student.universiteitleiden.nl/en/news/2026/09/can-ai-build-a-beautiful-city-in-minecraft-these-leiden-students-did-it-better?cf=science)).
- **Generated "Minecraft" with no engine.** Decart's Oasis (Oct 2024) was called a "nightmarish hallucination" ([TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/105436-oasis-interactive-ai-experience-turns-minecraft-nightmarish-hallucination.html)). Oasis 2.0 "goes haywire" the moment you place a block ([Sportskeeda](https://widgets.sportskeeda.com/minecraft/decart-announces-improved-minecraft-generative-ai-game-far-playable)).

**Hytale and AI.** I found no AI building feature shipped or announced. Simon Collins-Laflamme publicly warned Roblox creators in Feb 2026 that AI-first tools risk training on creators' work and then displacing them. He pitched Hytale as taking "as little as possible from creators (even 0%)" ([FinalBoss](https://finalboss.io/hytale-founder-warns-roblox-creators-ai-first-tools); [iXBT](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/02/09/rukovoditel-hytale-raskritikoval-kurs-roblox-na-generativnyi-ii.html)).

**Reading the reception as a whole:**
- **Platform-owner AI that advises or chats was mocked and shut down:** Copilot for Gaming, Merl.
- **Third-party AI that *does chores* found millions of users:** Player2.
- **Text-to-build stays a hobbyist niche.**
- **Benchmarks and the strongest academic competition both say the same thing:** LLMs are weak at raw 3D placement and good at code and structure.
- **The creator culture in both games is hostile to AI that replaces the act of creating.**

## 6. Complaints and gaps (builders, not fighters)

**Minecraft:**
1. **Missing shape variants,** decided by Mojang's taste ("limits creativity"): vertical slabs, mixed slabs, stairs and slabs for many materials (§2 sources).
2. **No vanilla bulk tools on Java, and nothing for console and mobile players** (Builder Mode requests; Editor is Windows-launcher only) ([feedback](https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/community/posts/46445679519757-Builder-Mode-for-Minecraft-Java-and-Bedrock); [Creator Tools](https://minecraft.net/en-us/creator/tools)). Teachers in Education ask for undo and clone ([Edu community](https://educommunity.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/community/posts/360072479032--UNDO-CLONE-type-commands-that-enable-teachers-to-quickly-build-edit-worlds-and-lessons)). *Undo* is missing from vanilla building.
3. **The edition split.** The best builder tools (Axiom, Litematica) are Java-only, the official look upgrade is Bedrock-only, and Axiom "breaks with OptiFine" ([Modrinth Axiom](https://modrinth.com/mod/axiom)).
4. **Look fragmentation and the cost of looking good:** shader performance, and Vibrant Visuals' tint and frame rate (§4).
5. **Marketplace problems:**
   - quality, and creators lifting Java mods ([Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/minecraft/minecraft-players-raise-concern-mod-ripoffs-marketplace)),
   - discoverability across 40,208 items ([MinecraftPal](https://www.minecraftpal.com/news/114/why-some-marketplace-packs-flop)),
   - a revenue cut that creators call excessive ([feedback](https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/community/posts/4419481296013/comments/4422017515661)),
   - the Marketplace Pass "slammed" at launch ([GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/minecraft-marketplace-pass-slammed-as-players-say-it-hits-a-new-low/)).
6. **Thin updates.** 26.1 and the Chaos Cubed drop were dubbed a "low-effort marketplace add-on" ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/survival/mojang-can-never-win-minecraft-fans-split-after-update-26-1-and-underwhelming-chaos-cubed-reveal-with-some-dubbing-it-a-low-effort-marketplace-add-on/)).
7. **Logic is opaque:** redstone is unexplained, and four separate languages for behaviour (§3).
8. **The creator economy trails Roblox,** and the "creator platform" has no specifics (§1).

**Hytale:**
- Thin content and direction (AOL, Switchblade).
- Tools still maturing: selection was cuboid-only at launch, crashes, tools without menus behind a help page of commands ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/hytale-creative-mode-explained-tools-worlds-and-what-you-can-build/); [mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/hytale-developers-show-27-minutes-of-creative-mode-already-featuring-elements-minecraft-fans-have-been-waiting-for-years,1539038)).
- No casual creative mode yet (official post).
- PC launcher only: no Steam, no consoles ([Shockbyte](https://shockbyte.com/blog/hytale-chapter-1-release-date)).
- "Not enough differences" (Bergensten).
- Analysts read Chapter I's guided structure as aimed at players who find Minecraft directionless ([tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/hytale-vs-minecraft-2026/)).

**The gap designers and press point at:** both games' building is either *survival chores* or *an editor*. Neither has a middle register where a casual player gets a good-looking, living place quickly. Hytale's own roadmap admits the hole (a "playful" creative mode), and so does Mojang's (a vague "creator platform").

## 7. Openings for a game like ours, and where we lose

**Where we can clearly win:**
- **(a) A higher-level grammar on a heightfield.** A Minecraft novice's box is a primitive problem: depth, pitched roofs, joined fences and graded paths all have to be learned. Smart blocks that form corners and connect, plus kit pieces, give the beginner the "good" grammar for free. Pros already leave Minecraft for heightfield tools (WorldPainter plus Gaea). Hytale's newest brushes ("grassy paths that blend", sculpt with erosion, gradient colour) move toward terrain-aware, intent-level tools. We have those *in game* (sculpt, paint, spline paths with grading, water poured into a hollow, meadow and tree brushes). That is the register both rivals are missing: fast and good-looking without being an editor.
- **(b) One coherent look.** Minecraft's look is split across edition, pack and shader. We can promise that every screenshot, every stamp shared between players and every LLM build sits in one style. Hytale proves an art bible can be held across props and mods. Write ours down to Hytale's level of rule (texel or edge rules, stretch limits, colour rules) so kit parts and future player parts stay inside it.
- **(c) An LLM co-builder writing the same edits as the hand.** The evidence points exactly at our architecture:
  - LLMs fail at raw voxels (GDMC 2026, VoxelCodeBench) and succeed at code and structure (MC-Bench).
  - The GDMC winners won with *templates placed by terrain-aware rules*. That is our "pieces, stamps, paths on a heightfield", with the LLM choosing and placing, not voxelising.
  - The ghost-first workflow already exists in the wild (AIbuilder to a Litematica projection; Hytale's prefab ghost with a layer-by-layer reveal), so players understand it.
  - Mojang and Hytale both refuse AI building. The space is open, but only for AI that leaves the player as author: one undo, editable afterwards, chores first.
- **(d) Small playable games in the same world.** Housing proves players want their builds to *do* something. Hytale's trigger volumes prove "shapes plus rules" is the reachable no-code layer. Minecraft's logic tiers are four languages, each harder than the last. "Create" (a game from a sentence) plus dungeons placed in the Home is the "unique solution" Bergensten said Hytale lacks.

**Where we lose, bluntly:**
1. **Audience and multiplayer.** Showing off is a core reason to build (Planet Minecraft, Build Battle, Housing visits, BuildTheEarth). We have no multiplayer, so the strongest loop is missing. A single-player Home is a diorama with no visitors.
2. **Need.** Survival gives a Minecraft base its meaning. Our unlocks-from-games are a weaker pull unless the games need what you build.
3. **Freedom of grain.** A 1 m cube lets anyone make *anything*: pixel art, redstone computers, a 1:1 Earth. Smart pieces trade that away. The vertical-slab war shows builders resent shape limits set by the developer's taste. Our grammar must not feel like Mojang saying "limits creativity" in reverse.
4. **Ecosystem.** 100 B mod downloads, 67 M WorldEdit downloads, Education, a $500 M Marketplace, 155 M MAU. We cannot match the scale or the mods, and should not try.

**The traps:**
- **The "too familiar" trap** (Hytale): polish plus a familiar grammar earned "I'd rather play Minecraft".
- **The editor-sprawl trap:** Hytale's dozens of tools serve creators and had to spawn a separate casual mode.
- **The AI backlash trap:** AI as adviser or author is mocked; AI as hands is welcome.
- **The single-look trap:** no shader escape hatch, so the look has to *be* good, at every hour, on modest PCs.
- **The LLM spatial-error trap:** measure every LLM build before it can be accepted, never trust it.
- **The thin-content trap:** Hytale's reviews show that a great toolset with little to *do* loses players within a month.

## Ten findings that should change our design

1. **The LLM should choose and place templates on terrain, never voxels.** GDMC 2026 winners: LLMs "struggle with reasoning about three-dimensional space"; templates placed by terrain rules won. Source: [Leiden](https://www.student.universiteitleiden.nl/en/news/2026/09/can-ai-build-a-beautiful-city-in-minecraft-these-leiden-students-did-it-better?cf=science).
2. **Treat spatial correctness, not code validity, as the LLM's failure point.** Check every ghost for overlaps, floating parts and blocked paths before it can be accepted. VoxelCodeBench: "executable code was far easier than spatially correct outputs". Source: [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/gpt-55-topped-a-minecraft-building-benchmark-and-the-spatial-reasoning-implications-go-far-beyond-gaming/).
3. **Ghost-first is the right pattern, and the ghost should reveal layer by layer** so the player watches it rise and can stop it. Sources: [AIbuilder to Litematica](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/aibuilder), [Hytale Update 6 prefab ghost API](https://hytale.com/news/2026/8/update-6-patch-notes).
4. **Market the LLM as the hands for chores, not as an adviser.** Copilot-in-Minecraft was mocked and killed in May 2026, while the chore-doing Player2 companion has 6.6 M downloads. Chores here: grade this path, fence this field, plant this slope. Sources: [Tom's Hardware](https://tomshardware.com/video-games/xbox/xbox-ceo-asha-sharma-kills-copilot-for-gaming), [CurseForge Player2](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/player2npc).
5. **Ship the thin shapes builders beg for** (vertical/half pieces, mixed corners, slabs for every material) in the smart-block grammar from day one. Minecraft's decade-long vertical-slab grievance shows taste-imposed limits are resented. Source: [Beebom](https://beebom.com/why-vertical-slabs-should-be-in-minecraft).
6. **Make our heightfield sculpt a headline feature and give it erosion, sampling and gradient paint.** Minecraft's terrain pros leave the game for heightfield tools, and Hytale is moving brushes that way. Sources: [WorldPainter workflow](https://polystruct.github.io/creative/worldpainter/generation.html), [Hytale Update 5](https://hytale.com/news/2026/5/update-5-patch-notes).
7. **Build the future behaviour layer for players as "shapes plus rules"** (trigger volumes with 20+ effects and conditions, saved inside stamps), with a text form the LLM can write. Housing's power users had to invent HTSL to get one. Sources: [Hytale Update 5](https://hytale.com/news/2026/5/update-5-patch-notes), [HTSL](https://housingdocs.github.io/html/HTSL/Introduction.html).
8. **Keep the hand toolset small and put the power in the sentence.** Hytale's editor-grade toolset forced it to plan a separate "playful" casual creative mode. Source: [Hytale Creative Mode](https://hytale.com/news/2025/11/hytale-creative-mode).
9. **A coherent look is necessary but not enough; differentiate on what the world does.** Hytale has the genre's best art bible and still drew "I'd rather just play Minecraft", so lead with games-in-the-Home and LLM building. Write our style bible to Hytale's precision. Sources: [Hytale modelling guide](https://hytale.com/news/2025/12/an-introduction-to-making-models-for-hytale), [Shazoo/Bergensten](https://shazoo.ru/2026/01/24/178608/kreativnyi-direktor-minecraft-poproboval-hytale-i-cestno-priznalsia-cto-predpocel-by-prosto-igrat-v-minecraft).
10. **Building needs an audience: make sharing and visiting early work, not a later polish item.** Planet Minecraft alone has 3.51 M submissions and rewards views and downloads, so give stamps and Homes a viewing surface before multiplayer exists. Keep human authorship visible (AI edits labelled, one undo), given creators' hostility to AI-first UGC. Sources: [Planet Minecraft](https://www.planetminecraft.com/members/levels/), [FinalBoss on Hytale vs Roblox AI](https://finalboss.io/hytale-founder-warns-roblox-creators-ai-first-tools).
