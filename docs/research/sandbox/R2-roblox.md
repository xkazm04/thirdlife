# R2: Roblox as a building sandbox (research, 27 Nov 2026)

Scope: Roblox Studio vs building inside experiences; parts, CSG, MeshParts, terrain; the Toolbox; Luau; the building-centred hits; the creator economy; Roblox's AI up to late November 2026; how young players learn; moderation limits on user content. Every claim carries a URL. [unverified] marks what I could not confirm from a primary or solid secondary source. Tracker numbers (Rotrends, RoWatcher) are third-party estimates.

Platform scale for context: Q3 2026 averaged 138 M DAU (down 9 % year on year) and almost 34 B hours engaged ([Q3 2026 shareholder letter, SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm)). Creators earned over $1.5 B in 2025, up from $923 M in 2024. The median DevEx creator got about $1,500 in the year to 30 June 2026, and over 42,000 creators are in DevEx ([Roblox, Sep 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/global-impact-of-creation-on-roblox)). The top 1,000 creators averaged $1.3 M in 2025 ([Naavik, State of UGC 2026](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)).

## 1. Why people build there

**Roblox has two kinds of building, and they hardly meet.** *Studio building* is developers making games in a desktop editor. *In-experience building* is players in a plot or base system that one game's developer wrote (Bloxburg, Theme Park Tycoon 2, Build A Boat). Most players never touch Studio. Evidence: 71 % of people who made a game with the new prompt tool Build had never used Studio ([RDC 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/rdc-2026-the-world-needs-more-play)), and the Q3 letter puts it at 75 % of 500,000 alpha users ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm)).

**Building is a big genre but never the top one.** Newzoo ranks "Roleplay & Avatar Sim" first by daily visits (116 M) and Simulation second (110 M) ([Newzoo via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-roblox-as-a-platform-in-2025)). The 2025 peaks were Grow A Garden (21.3 M concurrent users) and Steal A Brainrot (25.4 M concurrent) ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)). Both are collect-and-show-off loops on a personal plot, not construction. The building-centred hits by lifetime visits:

| Experience | What building is for | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome to Bloxburg (2014) | House = reward for working one of 14 jobs; life-sim roleplay | ~10.1 B visits, 14 M favourites ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/88070565/Welcome-to-Bloxburg)); 900 K+ peak concurrent at the June 2025 map revamp, about 14-16 K now ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/welcome-to-bloxburg-at-14k-players-can-roblox-s-building-icon-stage-a-comeback)) |
| Build A Boat For Treasure | Build a vessel, then survive a river of hazards to reach treasure | ~5.2 B visits ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/210851291/build-a-boat-for-treasure)) |
| Theme Park Tycoon 2 (2012) | Build a park that guests use; coasters, paths, flow | 1.6 B visits, 6.1 M favourites, 57.7 K peak concurrent ([Rotrends](https://rotrends.com/game/31970568/Theme-Park-Tycoon-2)) |
| Lumber Tycoon 2 | Owning land opens base building and sawmill automation in the late game | ~1.3 B visits ([fan wiki](https://lumber-tycoon-2.pages.dev/)) [unverified] |
| Adopt Me! | Houses bought and furnished; building system overhauled June 2025 | ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/adopt-me-furniture-storage-guide)) |
| Obby Creator | Pure creation: build obbies, others visit and like them | its developer says retention is "REALLY bad", so Roblox rarely recommends it ([update log](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/obby-creator-update-logs/372543)) |

**The loops that make building matter:**
- **Earned reward plus identity: Bloxburg.** "The house is the reward". Kids treat it as a design tool first, and it is the nearest thing on Roblox to The Sims ([Screenwise](https://screenwiseapp.com/media/bloxburg-game)). Player homes carry the game's identity on YouTube and TikTok through *speed builds*, and a single viral build can start a style trend ([Coohom blog](https://www.coohom.com/article/how-viral-bloxburg-builders-design-roblox-houses), opinion). A builder-for-hire market exists for "autobuilt" Bloxburg houses ([Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/burkhaard/autobuild-you-a-house-in-bloxburg)), so builds have real social value.
- **A game on top: Build A Boat, Theme Park Tycoon 2.** The build is tested by the world (the river, the guests). That gives novices a reason and a verdict.
- **Economy: Lumber Tycoon 2.** Building is the late-game sink for resources gathered by hand.
- **Sharing on its own does not hold players.** Obby Creator has likes and a "featured from the top 1,000" shelf and still retains badly ([log](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/obby-creator-update-logs/372543)).

**The cautionary tale is Bloxburg against Brookhaven.** Brookhaven is free roleplay that needs no building skill. It runs 300-500 K concurrent users daily, with 69 B+ visits. Bloxburg sits near 5 % of that. RoWatcher's diagnosis: Bloxburg's "steep learning curve", meaning hours of grinding money and learning mechanics before a presentable home, intimidates casual players. Its spikes "grab attention but cannot hold it". It suggests templates, assisted building and prebuilt starter homes ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/welcome-to-bloxburg-at-14k-players-can-roblox-s-building-icon-stage-a-comeback)). Roleplay won by removing the build step.

## 2. The building primitive

**Studio.** The atoms are Parts (block, wedge, cylinder, sphere, measured in studs), unions made by CSG ("solid modeling"), imported MeshParts, and voxel Terrain.
- **Parts are fast to start and slow at scale.** The look comes from alignment and many small parts. Builders have complained about drift and snapping since Studio 2013: "tiny offsets", model bounding boxes that break alignment, and a 2026 thread titled "Roblox just ruined building | Why precision is impossible" ([edge snapping request](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/part-edge-snapping/1736980); [precision thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-just-ruined-building-why-precision-is-impossible/4003468)). The Dragger QoL beta added a Shift toggle between grid snapping and soft snapping ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/dragger-qol-improvements-beta/2661668)).
- **CSG** got engine rewrites (2021, 2024-25) that cut triangles and failures ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/studio-solid-modeling-csg-performance-and-robustness-improvements/3256495)). Collision fidelity against performance is still a constant trade-off ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/common-union-and-mesh-misconceptions/998897)). "CSG on meshes" and "individually rounded corners" slipped from early to mid 2026 ([Spring 2026 roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-roadmap-2026-spring-update/4625473)).
- **Terrain** is built from 4-stud voxels. Users call it "a nightmare to work with". The rewritten Terrain Editor beta was called "extremely buggy and borderline unusable", and staff reverted its improvements ([Terrain Editor Update beta](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/terrain-editor-update-beta/2841125)). "Enhanced voxel terrain" was pushed from mid to late 2026 ([roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-roadmap-2026-spring-update/4625473)).
- **Escapes from tedium, community side.** The plugins builders name over and over are F3X Building Tools, GapFill, ResizeAlign, Archimedes (curves and arcs from parts) and Studio Build Suite ([DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/best-plugins-for-building-on-roblox-and-what-they-do/1364639); [Roblox 2017](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2017/05/3-must-studio-plugins)). I found no install counts. F3X was also the in-game building kit behind many "free build" places ([F3X wiki](https://github.com/F3XTeam/ROBLOX-Building-Tools/wiki)). The biggest escape is the **Toolbox and free models**. Free models carry a long-standing backdoor problem: obfuscated `require()` calls and hidden scripts copied across many assets ([DevForum: malicious café models](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/malicious-scripts-inside-cafe-models/3887121); ["why does the toolbox even exist?"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/dangerous-models-or-why-does-the-toolbox-even-exist/897662)). Roblox added a paid Creator Store in USD (plugins from $4.99, models from $2.99) ([docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/creator-store); [launch](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-store-buy-and-sell-plug-ins-with-real-world-currency/2923445)).
- **Escapes from tedium, Roblox side: Procedural Models (Studio beta, 28 Apr 2026).** A `ProceduralModel` instance rebuilds its contents when its Size or attributes change. A ModuleScript generator receives the parameters and writes into a target container. Assistant can write the generator from a text or image prompt (`/generate_procedural_model`, 50 per 24 h) ([announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/studio-beta-introducing-procedural-models-build-parametrized-3d-models-with-code-or-ai/4592056); [docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/parts/procedural-models)). These are **smart pieces**, and the community called them exactly that: "basically just smart models" (56 likes). Known limits: **manual edits inside the Generated folder are lost on regeneration**, models can't be packaged yet, and runtime use is meant to be "sparing".

**In-experience.** Every game builds its own grammar. Bloxburg draws walls on a grid, closes rooms, roofs a room once its walls are complete, and paints floors and walls from a curated catalogue ([Nerd Stash](https://thenerdstash.com/how-to-build-a-house-in-welcome-to-bloxburg-roblox)). **Precision is sold as gamepasses**: Advanced Placing (250 Robux, micro-nudge and free rotation), Multiple Floors (360), Transform Plus (600), Large Plot (250), Basements (100) ([Screenwise](https://screenwiseapp.com/media/bloxburg-game)). Off-grid placement without Advanced Placing "causes gaps" ([fan guide](https://welcome-to-bloxburg.pages.dev/guides/build-mode-improvements/), [unverified]). Dense decorated plots have historically dropped frame rates in build mode (same guide). Persistence runs on DataStores, at most about 4 MB per key and 4 MB/min of writes per key ([docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/cloud-services/data-stores/error-codes-and-limits)). Every plot game designs around that ceiling.

**Novice speed.** In Studio, a novice gets a template obby quickly. Roblox's own "Intro to building" course now starts from the Classic Obby template and has the learner change part behaviour with **AI Assistant code** ([curriculum](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/building)). In Bloxburg, a presentable home takes "hours of money grinding and learning complex mechanics" ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/welcome-to-bloxburg-at-14k-players-can-roblox-s-building-icon-stage-a-comeback)).

## 3. The behaviour layer

**Luau in Studio is the whole behaviour layer, and it is developer-only.** Players inside experiences never script. Behaviour reaches them as pre-made parts the developer exposes: hinges, wheels, motors and cannons in Build A Boat ([mech guide](https://roblox-build-a-boat-for-treasure-mech.pages.dev)), engineering physics in Plane Crazy ([Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/beginner-s-guide-roblox-plane-crazy)), rides and guest pathing in Theme Park Tycoon 2.

**Who scripts.** A CHI 2025 study interviewed 18 teen Roblox developers aged 14-19. 15 scripted, 7 modelled and 5 built ([arXiv 2502.18120](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.18120v1)). They learned mostly from **Discord servers, the DevForum and peers**, with one saying about 40 % of what they knew came from communities. Beginners used ChatGPT for basic code, but "most said it couldn't replace community knowledge". Barriers they named: invite-only servers, jargon, judgemental answers, school pressure, Roblox being seen as "childish". Harms they named: unpaid commissions, **code and asset theft**, no contracts. Forum talk says scripting commissions pay far more than building ones ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/what-is-a-fair-price-to-pay-game-builders-and-scripters-in-robux-or-usd/3027798), anecdotal).

**What AI does to the barrier.** The Build numbers (71-75 % never opened Studio) say a prompt tool reaches people Luau never did. The most-liked reply under the February 2026 MCP announcement (149 likes) worried that AI will stop beginners learning to code and debug ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/studio-mcp-server-updates-and-external-llm-support-for-assistant/4415631)).

**Behaviour attached to generated objects.** 4D generation's "schemas" (Car-5: a body plus four named wheel MeshParts; Body-1: any single mesh) split a generated object into named parts. Pre-written scripts are then *retargeted to the object's dimensions* ([Roblox, Feb 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/02/accelerating-creation-powered-roblox-cube-foundation-model)). In other words, behaviour comes from a fixed library of rigs and the AI only fills the shape. A "Custom Schema and Behavior Library" was slated for mid 2026 ([roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-roadmap-2026-spring-update/4625473)).

## 4. The look

- **No house style; "Roblox" is a default.** Parts went smooth and stud-less, and the Surface tool was removed in August 2020. Studs came back only as optional material textures ([Roblox wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Surface); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/deprecating-surface-tool-api/1222218)). Since then the platform has added Future lighting, PBR MaterialVariants, 4K textures, emissive masks and mesh/texture streaming ([roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-roadmap-2026-spring-update/4625473)). The look is whatever each developer assembles. Mixing free models from many authors gives the familiar clash. The community looks down on heavy free-model use ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/free-synty-asset-packs-released-in-the-marketplace/1283755)), and Roblox's answer was to promote style-coherent packs: free Synty packs "share a similar art style, so they can be mixed and matched" (same thread).
- **Where coherence holds.** Inside a single curated game (Bloxburg's catalogue, Theme Park Tycoon 2's pieces), player builds sit together well because one developer drew every piece. That last point is my inference.
- **What AI does to the look.** Observers can now spot AI-built games by their defaults: a grey baseplate, floating text UI, an unstyled shop button, generic particles ([Altitude DP, Sep 2026](https://altitudedp.com/research/ai-slop-is-flooding-roblox-and-thats-fine)). On 4D output, PCGamesN wrote that cars and planes look "decent enough" but organic characters move awkwardly and "just look a bit shit" ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/roblox/4d-generative-ai-model-update)).
- **The hardware ceiling.** About 60 % of players are on devices with 2-4 GB of RAM ([Roblox docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/test-on-hardware)), and mobile is about 80 % of DAU ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/80-of-roblox-users-are-on-mobile-contributing-46-of-robux-revenue)).
- **Players fighting the look.** PC players used ReShade and Bloxshade (via NVIDIA Ansel). Hyperion anti-cheat blocked the overlays in 2023, and 2025 changes broke Ansel ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-security-changes-break-nvidia-ansel-vulkan-layer-support/3601172); [Roonby](https://roonby.com/2025/04/29/roblox-shaders-are-getting-banned-whats-going-on/)). A FastFlag allowlist followed in September 2025 ([GitHub README](https://github.com/ThunderBoltCODMYT/My-JSON-for-Roblox-Rivals), [unverified]). Roadmap replies still ask for RTX lighting and emissive maps for user-made avatar items ([roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-roadmap-2026-spring-update/4625473)).

## 6. Complaints and gaps (builders, not combat players)

1. **Engine neglect for AI.** Voxel terrain, CSG on meshes and rounded corners keep slipping while AI ships. Developers ask for water as a material and better terrain ([roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-roadmap-2026-spring-update/4625473); [MCP thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/studio-mcp-server-updates-and-external-llm-support-for-assistant/4415631)).
2. **Terrain tools.** 4-stud voxels, a reverted editor rewrite, and no fill-with-air option ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/terrain-editor-update-beta/2841125)).
3. **Precision and snapping drift** since 2013 ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/add-a-snap-to-grid-context-action-to-restore-minor-displacements-while-building/477648)).
4. **Free-model risk and theft.** Toolbox backdoors, plus copied games and plagiarised assets ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.18120v1)). Roadmap replies fear the copycat-moderation system will also hit legitimate "inspired" games.
5. **In-experience skill floor and paywalls.** Bloxburg's precision passes and money grind ([Screenwise](https://screenwiseapp.com/media/bloxburg-game); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/welcome-to-bloxburg-at-14k-players-can-roblox-s-building-icon-stage-a-comeback)).
6. **Builds are trapped.** A Bloxburg house lives in Bloxburg's DataStore (4 MB per key). Avatar items made inside an experience can't be listed on the Marketplace, resold or traded ([docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/avatar/in-experience-creation); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/client-beta-in-experience-creation-for-accessories-and-layered-clothing/4037419)). Nothing a player builds travels between games.
7. **Moderation exposure of building games.** Developers fear the whole game will be taken down for one offensive player build. Standard mitigations are prefab-only parts, upvote-or-friends visibility and in-game reports ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/with-placement-systems-in-games-how-are-inappropriate-builds-dealt-with/550531)). Roblox runs "Violative Scenes Detection" and RM3, a real-time multimodal scene moderation system ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/strengthening-our-safety-policies-and-tools/3882864); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm)). **Free-form creation (drawing, writing, pixel grids) is limited to players aged 16+.** Builds assembled from 3D assets that were already moderated, "such as building a house", are explicitly *not* free-form ([content maturity docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity.md)). Roblox Kids (5-8) and Select (9-15) catalogues exclude free-form drawing and social hangouts by default ([Business Wire, Jun 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260616748440/en/)). Generated content through Roblox's APIs is moderated on input and output and targets All Ages. Continuous chatbot-style AI requires an 18+ Restricted rating ([generative AI docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/generative-AI)).
8. **Safety climate.** Many US state suits and settlements in 2025-26, country bans, and mandatory age checks for chat. 64 % of DAU were age-checked by the end of Q3 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_safety_on_Roblox); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068235/ex991-q32026earningsshar.htm)). Every social or creative feature now ships behind age gates.
9. **Discovery for new creations.** Ranking by retention protects against slop but starves new titles of the data they need. Roblox has described no exploration path for generated games ([Gaming Intelligence](https://gamingintelligence.ai/news/roblox-build-mobile-ai-alpha)).

## 7. Openings for a game like ours, and where we lose

**Where we can clearly beat Roblox**

- **One grammar instead of one per game.** On Roblox every plot game reinvents building (Bloxburg's walls, Theme Park Tycoon 2's paths, Build A Boat's blocks), and Studio parts are a separate, professional grammar. Our smart blocks, kit pieces, heightfield and stamps are one grammar shared by the Home and the games. Procedural Models show Roblox moving towards smart parametric pieces, but they are Studio-only and edit-time, and **regeneration throws away manual edits**. Our design (an LLM ghost accepted as one undo, then hand-editable) is exactly the property they lack.
- **An AI co-builder for players, not developers.** Roblox's AI serves developers (Assistant, Scene Gen), whole-game prompting (Build) or single wished objects (4D). Wish Master's +64 % playtime is weak evidence (one developer's claim), but it says players love saying a thing into the world. Nobody gives a player in a house plot "add a porch and a path to the lake" as editable kit operations.
- **A coherent look is a moat against slop.** AI-built Roblox games are recognisable by grey baseplates and mismatched assets. If every LLM edit can only place our cel-styled pieces, generated output cannot look off-style. That is the opposite of Cube meshes.
- **Safety by construction.** Roblox's own policy treats "assembled from pre-moderated 3D assets" as the safe class, below the 16+ free-form tier. A grammar of kit operations, with no free meshes, drawing or text on surfaces, lands in that class. Moderating a sentence is far easier than moderating a generated mesh (the Twin Towers episode).
- **Building that the world uses.** The building hits that last are the ones the world tests: Build A Boat's river, Theme Park Tycoon 2's guests, Lumber Tycoon 2's economy. Pure sharing (Obby Creator) and builds gated by skill (Bloxburg against Brookhaven) lose. Our dungeons in the same valley, plus unlocks earned from game results, are that test, provided the Home *affects* the games and not only displays trophies.
- **The skill floor.** RoWatcher's prescription for Bloxburg ("house templates, assisted building modes, prebuilt starter homes") is what an LLM co-builder and stamps give for free.

**Where we lose, bluntly**

- **People.** Roblox is a social network first: 138 M DAU, 12-player Bloxburg servers, friends visiting plots, YouTube speed-build culture. Showing off is the strongest building motive there, and we have no multiplayer and no audience.
- **Economy.** $1.5 B a year to creators, 42 K DevEx members, a commission market. We have nothing for the teen who builds for money and recognition.
- **The behaviour layer.** Luau lets anyone make *any* game. We have no player logic layer, so a "machine" builder (Plane Crazy, Build A Boat mechs) has nothing to do in our world.
- **Reach.** Roblox runs on 2-4 GB phones and is about 80 % mobile. A Godot cel look with heightfield and water is a PC-first experience.
- **Roblox ships the same pieces, fast.** Procedural Models (April), Scene Generation with editable terrain and models (October), Build with a Studio hand-off, 4D in-experience, and a "Custom Schema and Behavior Library". If Roblox puts Scene Generation and procedural models inside experiences, a Bloxburg-like game could have a co-builder within a year. Our edge has to be the *quality and coherence* of the grammar and look, not the presence of AI.
- **Traps:**
  - (a) AI replacing the craft. Roblox's own forum treats AI as a threat to the people who enjoy building ("demotivating for creators who want to make real art"), so the co-builder must leave the fun parts to the hand.
  - (b) A creation tool with no game. Obby Creator's retention is the warning.
  - (c) A skill wall. Bloxburg lost to Brookhaven.
  - (d) A style lock that some builders hate. Roblox builders keep asking for RTX lighting and realism, and we must accept losing that audience.
  - (e) AI spending starving the engine. Roblox's roadmap delays drew the loudest complaints, and our equivalents are terrain, water and snapping.

## Ten findings that should change our design

1. A plot needs a game on top: the building hits that last are tested by the world (Build A Boat's river, Theme Park Tycoon 2's guests), while pure sharing retains "REALLY bad". Make the Home feed the dungeons, not only display trophies. ([Obby Creator log](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/obby-creator-update-logs/372543); [Rotrends TPT2](https://rotrends.com/game/31970568/Theme-Park-Tycoon-2))
2. A skill floor kills building games: no-build Brookhaven runs about 20× Bloxburg's concurrent players, and the prescribed fix is templates, assisted building and starter homes. Ship a ready Home and make "say it" the first build verb. ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/welcome-to-bloxburg-at-14k-players-can-roblox-s-building-icon-stage-a-comeback))
3. Roblox's Procedural Models lose manual edits on regeneration; keeping hand edits on top of LLM or parametric output is our clearest technical edge, so treat it as a hard invariant. ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/studio-beta-introducing-procedural-models-build-parametrized-3d-models-with-code-or-ai/4592056))
4. Roblox has no AI co-builder for a player's own plot (Studio, Build and 4D serve other jobs); in-world wishing made Wish Master players play 64 % longer (developer claim). Put the LLM in the player's hands inside the world. ([Roblox Feb 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/02/accelerating-creation-powered-roblox-cube-foundation-model))
5. Roblox calls "assembled from pre-moderated 3D assets" safe and limits free-form creation to 16+; keep the LLM writing kit operations only, never free meshes or surface text, and safety becomes a selling point. ([content maturity docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity.md))
6. AI-built Roblox games are recognisable by default assets; a single enforced style means LLM output cannot look like slop, so never let generation step outside the kit's look. ([Altitude DP](https://altitudedp.com/research/ai-slop-is-flooding-roblox-and-thats-fine))
7. Bloxburg sells precision (Advanced Placing, Transform Plus) as gamepasses; give fine placement to everyone, and make unlocks about new pieces, never about basic control. ([Screenwise](https://screenwiseapp.com/media/bloxburg-game))
8. Builds and creations on Roblox cannot travel between games or be traded; let stamps and Home pieces move between the Home and the games as items. ([avatar in-experience creation docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/avatar/in-experience-creation))
9. Roblox's loudest builder complaints are terrain, water and snapping that slip behind AI work; budget engine feel (heightfield sculpt, water, snapping) ahead of new AI features. ([Spring 2026 roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-roadmap-2026-spring-update/4625473))
10. Roblox's agents were reported "hallucinating... wrong luau syntax" and staff warn against trusting the playtest agent alone; a narrow edit vocabulary plus a ghost-then-accept step is the right shape for a reliable co-builder, so do not widen it to free code. ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/studio-beta-studio-assistant-mcp-playtest-agent/4566767?page=2))
