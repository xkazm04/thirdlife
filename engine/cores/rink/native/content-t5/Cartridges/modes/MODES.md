# Director's choices: the twelve mode cartridges, and two ability modes without a card yet (lanes A67, A81 and A84, R-0036, R-0041)

Each file is a full `tj.cartridge/1` cut built from `hockey.regular` with only the keys below changed, plus `game.meta` (lane A81: the card's `pitch` and up to three
`facts`; the card's name is the envelope `title`). The menu lists them in the "Director's choices" rows of Choose your cut, three rows of four (`UJamMenuFlow::ModeCards`,
`MenuFlow.cpp`, which keeps only the order and reads the words from the file), chosen like a cut (`LoadCut("modes/hockey.mode.<slug>")`); the match card's line names the mode.
They are not in the cut list: `ListCuts()` scans only `Content/Cartridges/*.cartridge.json`. Proof: `node tools/playtest/modes.mjs` (numbers in
`docs/experiments/MODES.md`, `MODES-2.md`, under the vision `MODES-3.md`, the passing modes, the grace keys and the Marathon of legs in `MODES-4.md`, the twelve on the set ladder with the whistle out of eleven in `MODES-5.md`, five a side, the Marathon's puck and Arsenal's tricks in `MODES-6.md`, and the twelve on main ca493723 with Sniper's five against four in `MODES-7.md`). Validate: `node schema/validate.mjs Game/Content/Cartridges/modes/<file>`; the C++ loader runs them in `Jam.Core.CartridgeRoundTrip`
and `Jam.Menu.DirectorsChoices`.

**The whistle** (R-0048, R-0059; lane A149): `hockey.regular` plays with `rules.penalties` false, and so does every mode except Glow Ice (Glass House until lane A197), whose card says so. A new mode
is cut from `hockey.regular` and inherits that; a mode that wants the whistle is a second Glass House and needs a reason. **Levels** (lane A149): on the set ladder the
regular class wins 99 % of regular games at rookie, so no mode carries rookie: eleven are pro and Sudden Death is all_star.

**Team sizes** (lane A156): the regular game is five a side and the brain's shapes run for a team of five. Eight modes give the human a five (Arsenal, Bruisers, Glass House,
Pond, Shotgun, Sudden Death; Power Play 5 v 3; Sniper 5 v 4), Cage is four, Outnumbered 3 v 5, Marathon three, Fog of War three (its open ice) and Sniper five against four (lane A173: its lane and its passes). In a mode at five a side the human
passes 2.4 to 3.7 times a play minute (the regular game 2.8); at three a side 0.2 to 0.8.

**The drill's mate** (lane A173): the acceptance drill takes as its mate the first blue skater that is not a `back` and as its opponent the first orange
skater that is not a `back` (`HokejAcceptDrill.cpp:103-104`) and crashes without one (`:341`). Every mode gives the human a chaser or a support, and every
opposing side has a chaser: keep it so in a new mode.

Adding a mode: write the file here with a `game.meta` (pitch, up to three facts of at most 40 characters); the menu picks it up after the twelve in file order
(add its slug to the order in `UJamMenuFlow::ModeCards` to place it, and note the rows hold twelve at four a row); add its character number to `CHARACTER` in
`tools/playtest/modes.mjs` and prove it differs from regular by a third. Parked by lane A81: Tap-In (`tools/playtest/out/a81-variants/parked-tap-in.cartridge.json`, MODES-2.md).
